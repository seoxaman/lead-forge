FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where does the lead data come from?⌄
All leads are pulled live from Google My Business (GMB) profiles via Apify's Google Maps Scraper. The data is real, verified, and refreshed on every search.
What does "No website" mean exactly?⌄
A business is marked "No website" when their GMB profile doesn't have a website link. These businesses are perfect prospects for web designers and agencies offering web development services.
Do credits expire?⌄
No. Credits never expire. You can buy them once and use them whenever you want.
Can I filter by country and city?⌄
Yes. LeadForge supports 50+ countries and unlimited cities. You can filter by category, rating, review count, and more.
Is there a free trial?⌄
Yes! When you sign up, you get 10 free credits to test the platform. No credit card required.
Can I get a refund for bad leads?⌄
Yes. If a lead's phone and email both turn out invalid, we'll refund your credit automatically. Quality is guaranteed.
